Meet Lumi.

A small poster maker for a thought worth sharing: a quote, an announcement, a reminder to yourself. Add your words, choose a sculptural finish and export your artwork.

Designed to feel like glass.

Translucent surfaces, soft blur and light-catching edges, with a solid-surface fallback and reduced-motion support.

A working portfolio concept.

Prepared for Janina Kildisyte’s UI/UX portfolio with Codex assistance in concept, interface and implementation. User research is a next step; no measured usability outcomes are claimed.

Your collection stays with you.

Saved artworks are stored in this browser. There is no account or cloud sync. Exports are generated on your device.
